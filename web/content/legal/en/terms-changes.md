Version 0.4 of the terms of use replaces version 0.3, and the safety protocol is now version 0.2. Everything that changes describes how Rondje Mee already works. This is what changes:

- Live location can be switched on or off. As long as the data protection impact assessment (DPIA) is not finished, it is off for everyone. Even when it is on, your phone only shares your location during a walk alone with the dog, and only with the owner. Never during a first meeting or a group walk. Version 0.3 still said that your location was shared on every walk (sections 11 and 13).
- You can only ask for, accept or start a walk alone with the dog while live location is on. A fixed weekly walk goes with such a walk alone with the dog. While live location is off, neither is possible (section 6).
- We no longer promise an alert when a walk runs late. Rondje Mee sometimes sends one, but not always (section 13, and section 2 of the safety protocol).
- The SOS screen does not send anything to the owner or shelter by itself and does not share your location. If a dog runs off, you call the owner or shelter straight away and tell them where you last saw the dog. The text said you could select "dog escaped" in the app, but there is no such button (section 13, and sections 2 and 3.1 of the safety protocol).
- Section 4 now says exactly who sees what about you. On the page of a dog that belongs to an owner, only members (signed in, with a finished profile and not blocked) see the owner's first name, photo and text, the regular times and the dog's location to about 500 metres. Anyone who is not a member only sees the dog, the town and that there is an owner nearby. Rondje Mee has worked like this since 5 October 2026 (section 4).
- The safety protocol now also says what an owner does when the walker cannot be reached and there is no live map, and that we only keep a route if live location was on (sections 3.5 and 7 of the safety protocol).
- The terms and the safety protocol are still a draft: a lawyer has not reviewed them yet.

## Version 0.3 (replaces 0.2)

Did you accept a version from before 0.3? Then this changes as well, from version 0.3:

- Rondje Mee is provided by Laurens Bos, the maker of Rondje Mee. You reach us at the same contact address as before (article 1).
- Once a request is accepted, you both see each other's phone number and email address. If you sign up for a group walk, the shelter sees your phone number straight away. Rondje Mee has worked like this since 2 October 2026, but version 0.2 only mentioned the phone number after an accepted request. Version 0.3 describes it as it works (articles 4 and 6).
- You can support Rondje Mee with a voluntary gift through the fundraiser on Whydonate. The money goes to Laurens Bos for the costs of Rondje Mee, and he passes 10% on to good causes for dogs and people in the neighbourhood. A gift gives no priority and no tax benefit (article 8).
- The rule on our liability still mentioned a maximum amount that was never filled in. That maximum is gone. As far as the law allows, we remain liable only for direct damage caused by our own failure (article 15).
- We respond to a complaint within 14 days. That was a proposal and is now a promise (article 18).
- Notes to ourselves have been taken out of the text (articles 12 and 15). The rules themselves do not change because of it. The terms are still a draft: a lawyer has not reviewed them yet.
