Version 0.3 of the terms of use replaces version 0.2. This is what changes:

- Rondje Mee is provided by Laurens Bos, the maker of Rondje Mee. You reach us at the same contact address as before (article 1).
- Once a request is accepted, you both see each other's phone number and email address. If you sign up for a group walk, the shelter sees your phone number straight away. Rondje Mee has worked like this since 2 October 2026, but version 0.2 only mentioned the phone number after an accepted request. Version 0.3 describes it as it works (articles 4 and 6).
- You can support Rondje Mee with a voluntary gift through the fundraiser on Whydonate. The money goes to Laurens Bos for the costs of Rondje Mee, and he passes 10% on to good causes for dogs and people in the neighbourhood. A gift gives no priority and no tax benefit (article 8).
- The rule on our liability still mentioned a maximum amount that was never filled in. That maximum is gone. As far as the law allows, we remain liable only for direct damage caused by our own failure (article 15).
- We respond to a complaint within 14 days. That was a proposal and is now a promise (article 18).
- Notes to ourselves have been taken out of the text (articles 12 and 15). The rules themselves do not change because of it. The terms are still a draft: a lawyer has not reviewed them yet.
