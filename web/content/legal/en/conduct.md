# Rondje Code of Conduct

Rondje only works if everyone feels safe: walkers, owners, shelters and, above all, the dogs. This code of conduct is part of the Terms of Use. It applies to everyone who uses Rondje.

## 1. For everyone

- **Be honest.** About who you are, what you can do and, if you are an owner or shelter, about your dog.
- **Keep your appointments.** Can't make it? Cancel through the app as soon as possible.
- **No money.** Never ask for or offer money, tips, gift cards or any other payment for a walk.
- **Follow local rules**, for example on leashes, cleaning up after the dog, and where dogs are or are not allowed.
- **If in doubt, safety first.** It is better to cancel a walk than to take a risk.

## 2. Animal welfare during the walk

**Leash**

- The dog stays on the leash. Letting the dog off the leash is only allowed if the owner or shelter has explicitly permitted it, *and* it is legally allowed in that place.
- Use the leash and harness provided by the owner or shelter. Check that everything is securely fastened before you set off.
- Keep the leash short near traffic, other dogs, children and cyclists.

**Heat and cold**

- In warm weather, put your hand on the asphalt for 7 seconds. Too hot for your hand? Then it is too hot for paws.
- When it is hot, walk early in the morning or in the evening, look for shade and keep the walk shorter.
- Watch for signs of overheating: heavy panting, drooling, staggering or refusing to walk. Stop, find shade, give water and call the owner.
- In cold weather, watch out for ice and road salt. Rinse the paws after the walk if the owner asks you to.

**Water and pace**

- Bring water, or use what the owner provides, especially on longer walks.
- Adjust your pace to the dog. Stick to the agreed length of the walk.

**Treats and food**

- Only give treats if this is allowed, and only the treats listed on the profile or provided by the owner.
- Never give the dog your own food. Chocolate, grapes, raisins, xylitol (found in chewing gum) and onions are poisonous to dogs.
- Watch what the dog eats off the ground.

**Other dogs, people and children**

- Always ask the other person before dogs meet. Not every dog wants contact.
- Only let children or other people pet the dog if the owner is fine with it. Always stay close.
- If you notice the dog getting stressed (ears flat, tail tucked, growling), calmly move away.

**Traffic and darkness**

- Cross at safe places. Keep the dog on the inside of the pavement, away from the road.
- Do not use an extendable leash along busy roads.
- Make sure you are clearly visible in the dark, for example with a light or reflective gear.

**Cleaning up**

- Always pick up the poop with a bag and put it in a bin.

**Never**

- leave the dog alone or tie it up, for example outside a shop;
- hand the dog over to someone else;
- bring other dogs along without permission;
- take the dog in a car, on a bike or into someone else's home, unless the owner has explicitly agreed;
- punish, hit, yank or frighten the dog.

## 3. For owners

- Give an honest picture of your dog, including the difficult sides. Always mention any previous bites or aggression.
- Provide a suitable collar or harness, a strong leash, poop bags and anything else you promise on the profile.
- Do not hand over your dog if it is ill, injured or in heat, unless you have discussed this with the walker in advance.
- Explain what the walker needs to know: commands, fears, medication, the dog's favourite routes.
- A walker is a volunteer, not staff. Do not ask for more than what was agreed.

## 4. For shelters

- Assess each dog carefully and choose a suitable experience level.
- Give walkers a short briefing before a group walk and stay with the group.
- Do not take on more walkers than you can safely supervise.
- Also follow the Partner Terms for Shelters.

## 5. Respect between people

- **No harassment.** No unwanted advances, no sexual comments, no threats. No means no.
- **No discrimination.** Everyone is welcome on Rondje, regardless of background, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age (18+).
- **Respect privacy.**
  - Use phone numbers and addresses only for the walk.
  - Do not share other people's details.
  - Do not copy or photograph anyone's identity document.
- **Photos.** Only post photos or videos of the dog or of other people online with their permission, or the owner's. Never show where the owner lives.
- **Contact outside Rondje.** Keep contact about the walk. Do not keep contacting someone who does not want it.

## 6. Zero tolerance

We take immediate action on the following, often with a permanent ban:

- animal abuse or neglect;
- violence or threats;
- sexual harassment;
- scams, or asking for money or bank details;
- fake profiles or pretending to be someone else;
- discrimination or hate speech.

## 7. What happens if you break this code?

1. Someone reports it, or we notice it ourselves.
2. We look into the report. Where needed, we ask both sides for their side of the story.
3. We choose a measure that fits the seriousness: a warning, a restriction (for example, no more solo walks), a temporary suspension or a permanent ban.
4. We explain our decision, and you can appeal.

In the case of criminal offences or danger to people or animals, we may involve the police or other authorities.

Seen something that isn't right? Report it in the app. If there is immediate danger, call **112** first.
