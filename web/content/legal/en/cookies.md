# Rondje Mee Cookie Statement

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website places on your device. Apps and websites can also store data in your device's local storage. In this statement, we refer to all of this as "cookies and local storage".

## 2. Only our own cookies, no tracking

Rondje Mee **only uses its own cookies**: to make Rondje Mee work, and to let an invitation count (see the table in section 3). We do not use cookies or similar technologies for:

- advertising;
- tracking your browsing behaviour, including on other websites;
- visitor profiles;
- social media buttons that track you.

We do count visits to the website, but without cookies. See section 5.

Functional cookies do not require consent. That is why you will not see a cookie banner on Rondje Mee. The law allows this in the Netherlands (art. 11.7a Telecommunications Act), Belgium (art. 10/2 of the Act of 30 July 2018 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data) and Spain (art. 22.2 LSSI). If we ever start using other cookies, we will ask for your consent first.

## 3. Which cookies do we use?

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `__Secure-better-auth.session_token` | Keeping you signed in and securing your account | 7 days; renewed while you use Rondje Mee, and removed when you sign out |
| `better-auth.state`, `better-auth-passkey` | Security while signing in with Google, Apple or a passkey | A few minutes |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remembering which language you use Rondje Mee in | 1 year |
| `rondje_ref` | Only if you came through an invite link or a sign-up link with a code: the code from that link. If you create an account, your sign-up counts for the person who invited you, or for the poster or campaign the link was on. Only the code, nothing about you | 30 days |
| `rondje_later` | Which step of One thing now on Today you put away with "Later" (a week; after twice, for good) or "No, not now", so the page shows the right step straight away. We do not store it anywhere | 1 year |

## 4. Local storage on your device

Rondje Mee stores some things only on your own device, in the browser or in the app:

- **Mood check-ins.** How you feel before and after a walk. This data stays on your device and never reaches Rondje Mee.
- **A question for later.** If you choose "Later" when we ask about notifications or about putting Rondje Mee on your home screen, your browser remembers for two weeks not to ask again yet. If you choose "It's already there", it remembers that for a year.
- **The dog school.** Without an account, your browser remembers which lessons you finished (`rondje.lessons`); with an account they move to your account and are removed here. During the safety quiz, your browser remembers which question you were at until you close the tab (`rondje.quiz`), so you can carry on after a lesson.
- **Preferences**, such as whether you have sounds switched on or off (`rondje.sounds`), and which items of the checklist for a first meeting you have already ticked off.

You can delete this data yourself, for example by clearing the site data in your browser, or clearing the app data or deleting the app. Please note: your check-ins will then really be gone. We do not have a copy.

## 5. Visitor statistics without cookies

On the website we count visits with Vercel Web Analytics. This works without cookies: Vercel recognises a visit by a code calculated from your browser's request, and discards that code after 24 hours. We only see totals, such as which pages are viewed and which websites visitors come from. Admin pages are not counted, and nothing is counted in the iPhone and Android apps. Exactly what is sent to Vercel is described in section 8 of the Privacy Policy.

## 6. Maps from OpenStreetMap

To show maps, your device loads map images (tiles) from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers. Your IP address and technical data are then sent to those servers, just as with any website you visit. According to our information, OpenStreetMap does not set tracking cookies when doing so. You can read more about this in our Privacy Policy and in the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.

## 7. Deleting or blocking cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the session cookie, you will not be able to sign in and Rondje Mee will not work properly.
