# Rondje Cookie Statement

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website places on your device. Apps and websites can also store data in your device's local storage. In this statement, we refer to all of this as "cookies and local storage".

## 2. Functional cookies only

Rondje **only uses cookies that are needed to make Rondje work**. We do not use cookies or similar technologies for:

- advertising;
- tracking your browsing behaviour;
- statistics or analytics by other companies;
- social media buttons that track you.

Functional cookies do not require consent. That is why you will not see a cookie banner on Rondje. The law allows this in the Netherlands (art. 11.7a Telecommunications Act), Belgium (art. 10/2 of the Act of 13 June 2005 on electronic communications [to verify]) and Spain (art. 22.2 LSSI). If we ever start using other cookies, we will ask for your consent first.

## 3. Which cookies do we use?

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `__Secure-better-auth.session_token` | Keeping you signed in and securing your account | 7 days; renewed while you use Rondje, and removed when you sign out |
| `better-auth.state`, `better-auth-passkey` | Security while signing in with Google, Apple or a passkey | A few minutes |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remembering which language you use Rondje in | 1 year |
| `rondje_ref` | Remembering whose invite link you followed, so that person can see you joined [to verify: functional or consent needed] | 30 days |

## 4. Local storage on your device

Rondje stores some things only on your own device, in the browser or in the app:

- **Mood check-ins.** How you feel before and after a walk. This data stays on your device and never reaches Rondje.
- **A question for later.** If you choose "Later" when we ask about notifications, your browser remembers for two weeks not to ask again yet.
- **Preferences**, such as display options or settings you choose [to verify: which].

You can delete this data yourself, for example by clearing the site data in your browser, or clearing the app data or deleting the app. Please note: your check-ins will then really be gone. We do not have a copy.

## 5. Maps from OpenStreetMap

To show maps, your device loads map images (tiles) from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers. Your IP address and technical data are then sent to those servers, just as with any website you visit. According to our information, OpenStreetMap does not set tracking cookies when doing so [to verify]. You can read more about this in our Privacy Policy and in the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.

## 6. Deleting or blocking cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the session cookie, you will not be able to sign in and Rondje will not work properly.
